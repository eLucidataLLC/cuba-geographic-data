# Reference table licenses

**ODbL 1.0** — https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

`cuba_water_names_reference.csv` and `cuba_onei_river_correspondence.csv` incorporate the project’s Overture-derived name/association products and geographic context from administrative geometry. This applies to the combined tables, without claiming rights over individual public-domain facts.

NGA Geographic Names Server (https://geonames.nga.mil/), GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/, CC BY 4.0), and ONEI (https://www.onei.gob.cu/). Original source identifiers and spellings are retained.

Overture Maps Foundation (https://overturemaps.org/) and OpenStreetMap contributors (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Source attribution: https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/. Original per-feature source observations are retained where available.

HydroSHEDS v2: Lehner, B., Roth, A., Huber, M., Anand, M., and Thieme, M. (2022), A sharper look at the world’s rivers and catchments, Eos, 103. https://doi.org/10.1029/2022EO220167. Source: https://www.hydrosheds.org/products/hydrosheds-v2 (CC BY 4.0).

HydroLAKES v1: Messager, M.L., Lehner, B., Grill, G., Nedeva, I., and Schmitt, O. (2016), Estimating the volume and age of water stored in global lakes using a geo-statistical approach, Nature Communications, 7:13603. https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms13603. Source: https://www.hydrosheds.org/products/hydrolakes (CC BY 4.0).

Province context derives from the project administrative geometry product (ODbL 1.0), including Natural Earth public-domain data. ONEI relationships remain spelling-supported candidates, not confirmed river identity matches.
